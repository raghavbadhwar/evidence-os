# Field protocol · termination notice pilot

**Decision to make.** Does adding this workspace to commercial lawyers’ usual workflow produce fewer missed controlling exceptions and a faster answer a supervising lawyer can check? The decision is whether to fund a larger blinded comparison. No client receives study answers. The [pinned audit](audit.md) defines the tested scope and the change to freeze.

## Enrolment and custody

1. A participating practice supplies a consecutive log over a fixed intake period of closed or historical commercial matters with a real termination-notice question, identifiable party, route and operative date. Accept unfamiliar native documents, scans, amendment chains, side letters, missing referenced attachments and unresolved events. Require custodian permission, compatible environment, language and jurisdiction within the study lawyers’ competence. Exclude prior exposure for an individual reader, duplicates from the same transaction family, prohibited use and questions requiring legal research outside the chosen file-based task. Record every reason. Avoid selecting by model output, filenames, short length or ease of OCR.
2. Use 12 independent matters and one prespecified first question per matter. Aim for eight with one answer-changing, determinate exception, two straightforward and two with genuinely unresolved period or authority. Include both missing service and known service facts across strata. Select randomly within eligible strata from the intake log using a recorded seed. Use the intact pre-change and subsequent executed packets from four of the determinate matters for follow-ups. Record constraints and undersupply before launch. Enrichment gives enough opportunities to observe misses. Report intake prevalence separately. Each matter and both snapshots stay in one study partition.
3. Before size filtering record screened, permissioned, otherwise eligible, selected and excluded counts with reasons. Measure file size, canonical passage count and compact serialized bytes on approved infrastructure. Report number/otherwise eligible over each cap and over any cap, plus failed ingestion and unavailable source counts. Preserve eligible oversize cases in the assigned workflow with manual fallback. Never shrink a packet to make it fit. Record unmeasurable sizes explicitly.
4. Custodian freezes the actual as-of file and later snapshot separately, hashes originals and derivatives, and keeps the identity key off the study system. Use consistent matter-local aliases for entities, signatories, defined terms, former names, document IDs and references. Preserve chronology, date intervals, calendars, page order, cross-references, signatures' evidential status, marginalia, version relationships and meaningful filenames equally for both arms. Redact metadata, overlays and hidden OCR text. Prefer restricting access to approved originals if redaction would change meaning. A second lawyer reconciles every derivative and missing exhibit to the manifest and original page. Record fidelity failures and exclusions.
5. Governance lead signs off people, devices, storage, model endpoints and regions, retention, logs, export restrictions and deletion schedule. Matter opt-in follows written approval covering **both** processors and the comparator tools. No keys, raw contracts or reference labels enter the study repo. Keep gold, answer records and model-accessible packet storage separate. Use a fresh source set per assigned reader, with no pre-existing rules, cache or peer answers. Record snapshot hashes and model/build revisions.

## Independent reference and assignment

Two commercial lawyers, neither a task reader nor a builder, independently answer from the frozen source pages before any system output exists. For each first, unchanged and amended task, record period or permissible range/abstention, party and aliases, route, as-of date and effective interval, controlling and competing passage spans and execution evidence, operative reason, conditions, known/missing service facts, and which additional evidence would resolve uncertainty. Mark each decisive exception that changes the answer if ignored. Group alternative equivalent citations in advance. Label separately when the period is known and service remains unknown.

A third senior lawyer resolves disagreements with written reasons from the originals. Preserve independent opinions, initial agreement per field, adjudication and alternate defensible answers. If genuinely indeterminate, lock permissible qualified answers and abstentions. Keep them in workflow/time denominators, outside determinate exception denominators. Timestamp and hash the gold in restricted storage before model runs. A later reference correction needs a blinded reason, a version and regrading of both arms.

Recruit four practicing commercial lawyers across comparable experience bands. Assign every matter once per arm to different lawyers, each lawyer seeing six different matters, three per arm. Use [assignment.csv](assignment.csv). Randomly map lawyer identities and matters within strata to slots with a recorded seed before opening files. Place the eight exception matters in slots 1–8, clean in 9–10, indeterminate in 11–12. Ensure four prespecified longitudinal files are spread across readers. Align experience if possible and record imbalance. Keep each reader’s entire longitudinal case in one arm. Interleave first reviews in the indicated order; separate sessions and lock each response before opening the next packet. No reader, reviewer or trainer may disclose another arm’s work. Use one unrelated synthetic exercise per arm for familiarization. Record tools and permitted AI normally used, experience and familiarity before assignment.

## Task cards and clocks

| Stage | Identical prompt in each arm | What to time |
|---|---|---|
| First review, 12 matters × 2 | “As of [date], for [party] using [route], what minimum applies? Give a supervisor-checkable answer, pinpoint sources, competing terms, conditions, and anything we still need to establish about service.” | Start when the unopened packet and question are released. End when reader locks answer and citations or submits documented escalation. Count upload, OCR triage, search, reading, verification, corrections, retries and fallback. Capture provisional product output before any human correction. Log normal-workflow draft if available. |
| Prepare reuse, four matched matters × 2 | “Prepare whatever you would rely on if this question returns.” In the product, verify and save a scoped rule, or document why unsafe. Usual arm can retain its normal notes or do nothing. | Separate interval from first lock through usable artifact or recorded refusal. Include reviewer and operator labor. Charge once per matter. Never preload a rule supplied by the reference team. |
| Unchanged follow-up, same four × 2 | “Assume [stated conditions]. Would [N] calendar days satisfy the period for [party] on [date]? Can we establish when service occurred?” Choose N near the boundary in advance. | Release only after setup. Allow previous within-arm work. Time to locked sourced answer, including fallback if no rule. |
| Changed follow-up, same four × 2 | “Here is the updated file as of [date]. Reassess the same proposal and identify what now controls.” Release a genuine later executed amendment which changes the answer, preserving earlier versions. | Start at handoff of change, before upload. Record rejection of old rule, earliest visible stale warning, re-review, replacement rule if needed and final answer. Time all steps. A safe block alone does not complete the client question. |

Use the same source access, calculator, task output form, time cap and escalation access in both arms. Product users can return to usual tools. Record every handoff and helper’s active minutes. Capture active lawyer person-minutes, unattended system latency, and elapsed wall time separately. Include blocking waits in elapsed time and active supervision in lawyer time, avoiding double counts. At 45 minutes per task lock the disposition and record actual labor plus cap status. Retain cases with no reviewable answer as failures; for the primary time comparison assign at least 45 minutes to a noncompletion (or actual larger summed labor). Report completed-only times as sensitivity. Define reviewable as a signed conclusion or specific defensible uncertainty with cited available evidence and the next missing fact. A generic referral is a noncompletion. Log later work separately.

## Grading and analysis

Two graders independently see randomly ordered standardized answers with source locators and anonymized task IDs. Strip tool names, UI text, timestamps, reader and arm; preserve actual citations, omissions and reasoning. Score before linking times or revealing arms. Resolve discrepancies with the reference adjudicator, log suspected unblinding. Never use a model as the reference grader. Capture a separate restricted trace of canonical pages, dispatch passage IDs, proposal, verdicts, signed answer, reusable rule and amended disposition. If the actual dispatch trace is unavailable mark recall unknown for that stage.

For an approved live study run, the operator may enable `EVIDENCE_STUDY_TRACE=true` on the workspace server. A matter owner reads `GET /v1/source-sets/{mid}/audit`, finds the `provider_dispatch_trace` event matching the assessment ID and snapshot in its JSON `target`, and enters the event ID in `dispatch_id_manifest_ref`. For each call compare the ordered `passage_ids` with the frozen decisive passage groups; count a group once if any accepted member is present. Score Sol and Jev separately, including retries. A `response_read` on a successful HTTP request identifies passages in the outgoing request associated with a response. Whether the model used an exception is a separate judgment on its proposal. Treat transport failures as unconfirmed and record HTTP error statuses separately. Use the original assessment trace for cache hits. Study tracing is off by default; it stores stage, model, opaque passage IDs, HTTP outcome and elapsed time in the matter audit. It excludes request prose, document names, keys and raw responses. Keep the audit export with the restricted study team.

* **Primary safety outcome.** On prelabelled determinate first-review exception opportunities, count a miss when the signed workflow answer omits the exception or fails to apply its party/date/route effect. A correct number without recognition/citation of the exception misses. An uninformative abstention, timeout or fallback that fails to identify it also misses. Divide by **all assigned gold exception opportunities** in that arm. A correct manual recovery counts as caught in the final workflow and separately as missed by the system. Report each exception, numerator/denominator, paired both-miss and discordant cells and harmful definitive answers separately. Also report matters with any miss. Do not pool follow-ups with first reviews.
* **Primary time outcome.** Compare capped lawyer person-minutes to reviewable answer on all 12 first-review pairs. Report both arm medians, median of paired differences and median per-matter percentage saving, with raw times, fallback and noncompletion flags. Report setup, unchanged and changed review separately, plus paired cumulative first + setup + both follow-ups for four longitudinal matters. Record time saved before and after charging setup. Estimate break-even unchanged repeats as extra setup minutes divided by observed positive minutes saved per unchanged repeat, rounding up. Report no observed break-even when savings are nonpositive.
* **Evidence and harm.** Score recall of gold decisive passage groups at extraction, each provider dispatch, citation and signed answer (found/required, with all-found matter count). A pinpoint must resolve to the frozen source version and entail the claimed scope. Count wrong definitive or materially unsupported proposals before review and signed conclusions after review, both per definitive answer and per assigned task. Document signatures/assumptions, citations that support only the broad term, and unsupported assertions of service. Score appropriate abstentions / all gold indeterminate fields, and unnecessary abstentions / determinate fields. Grade period and service separately.
* **Coverage.** Report definitive period answers / all assigned tasks, and correct / definitive with coverage beside it. At comparable coverage compare on the same paired matters answered definitively by both arms; disclose excluded and one-arm-only matters and all-arm results. For an equal-coverage sensitivity, collect confidence before lock in both arms, rank definitive answers by confidence with preassigned slot tie-break and keep the top k per arm where k is the smaller count. Report the case mix and correct/k. Retain all assigned matters for primary outcomes.
* **Operations.** Report model calls, retries, dispatch failures, cache hits, usage and invoiced or version-priced spend including failed attempts, plus cost per correct completed task. Unknown usage stays unknown. Report upload/ingest, model and end-to-end latency separately. Record fallback/escalation reason, who resolved it and cost. Show raw data and descriptive medians/ranges by stage and arm; any p95 is descriptive. Use paired matter-level effects and counts, split by complexity, size and reviewer. Follow-ups are repeated observations. Twelve matters and four readers cannot establish population superiority, rare-harm rates or lawyer-independent effects.

## Decision rules set before enrolment

These are **assumptions** for a diagnostic pilot. The eight exception opportunities allow one miss to move the rate by 12.5 points; seeing none still permits a one-sided 95% binomial upper limit of about 31% under independent equal-risk sampling. Matched review of 12 files costs 24 first reviews and permits both conditions for each lawyer without rereading a file. Four longitudinal pairs expose setup and changed-file overhead. Use a new, larger, unselected holdout for any efficacy claim; the repo brief suggests 30–50 unseen matters as an initial diagnostic set. Plan its size from observed discordant pairs, time variation and a prespecified meaningful effect.

| Decision | Prespecified boundary |
|---|---|
| Stop current build / pause immediately | Any data exposure, unauthorized use, stale rule released after changed sources, or asserted actual service without adequate evidence in a signed product answer. Also stop expansion if product has ≥2 more final exception misses than usual care or median paired first-review time is ≥25% slower. Investigate before restarting. |
| Proceed to larger study only | All 12 pairs logged, ≥8 determinate exception opportunities, four paired longitudinal sequences. Product has ≤1/8 final exception misses and at least two fewer than usual care. Median paired first-review percentage saving ≥20%, ≥8/12 pairs faster, ≥3/4 cumulative sequences faster including setup and amended-file recovery. No critical stop, no increase in materially unsupported signed answers, product coverage within one task of or above usual care, all four stale cases blocked before new interpretation. |
| Revise / inconclusive | Any remaining result, incomplete logging, comparator floor (e.g., both arms miss zero), >25% of otherwise eligible files over packet limits, or similarly frequent manual fallback (>25% of assigned product first reviews). These feasibility limits veto proceeding. Report whether retrieval, reasoning, review cost, capacity or case mix explains the result. Freeze a new build and use fresh matters to retest. |

### Testing the optional second reader

Keep one frozen Evidence OS configuration for every lawyer in the main comparison. Before enrollment, the study lead can use two isolated, rule-free copies of each frozen packet to run the original and independent-reader configurations on identical scopes. Keep both sets of provisional outputs away from lawyers and reference authors. Blind the graders to configuration and record extra calls, time, tokens, exception misses, escalations and numeric-answer coverage per matter. This paired diagnostic measures a change in system proposals. Attributing lawyer time savings to the extra reader requires a separately counterbalanced lawyer comparison with unfamiliar files. Include manual reconciliation of both sets of citations in that clock. See the [routing rules, limitations and runnable benchmark](../first-time-contract-review.md#independent-exception-reader).
